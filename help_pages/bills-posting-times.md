# When a bill payment shows on your bill

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Posting times
Electricity and water payments reach the biller within 1 banking day. Internet, phone and cable payments reach the biller within 2 banking days. Credit card payments reach the bank within 3 banking days.

## Pay before the due date
Pay at least 3 days before your due date, so the payment reaches the biller in time. Halimbawa Money can't cancel penalties a biller charges for a late payment.

## If the biller says you haven't paid
Wait for the posting time above. If the biller still hasn't received it after that, contact support with the reference number.
