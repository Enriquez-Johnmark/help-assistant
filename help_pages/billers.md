# Which billers you can pay

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Kinds of billers
You can pay electricity, water, internet, phone, cable TV, credit cards, loans, insurance, schools and government agencies. The full list is in the app under Bills.

## A biller that isn't listed
If your biller isn't in the list, you can't pay it through Halimbawa Money yet. Tell support which biller you need; the list is updated every month.
