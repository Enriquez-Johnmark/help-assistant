# Verifying your ID

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Why you verify
A verified wallet can send money, pay bills and hold up to ₱100,000. An unverified wallet can only receive money and hold up to ₱5,000.

## How to verify
In the app, tap Verify, take a photo of your ID, then take a selfie. Most checks finish within 24 hours.

## If your verification is rejected
Make sure the whole ID is in the photo, it isn't blurry, and there is no glare. Take the selfie in good light, without a cap or glasses, and make sure your face matches your ID. You can try again up to 3 times a day.
