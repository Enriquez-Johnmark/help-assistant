# Branch hours

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Opening hours
Most branches are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Branches in malls follow mall hours, usually 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., every day.

## Holidays
Branches close on Christmas Day and New Year's Day. On other public holidays, most branches open from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

## Finding a branch
Use Branches in the app to find the nearest branch and its hours.
