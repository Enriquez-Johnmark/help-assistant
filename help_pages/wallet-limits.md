# Wallet limits

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How much your wallet can hold
A verified wallet can hold up to ₱100,000. An unverified wallet can hold up to ₱5,000.

## Monthly limit
A verified wallet can receive up to ₱100,000 each month. The limit starts again on the first day of the month.
