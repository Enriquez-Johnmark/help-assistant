# Getting your pawned item back

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How to redeem
Bring your pawn ticket and your ID to the branch where you pawned the item. Pay the loan, the interest and the service charge, and you get your item back.

## Someone else redeeming for you
Another person can redeem the item with your pawn ticket, a signed letter from you, a copy of your ID and their own ID.

## Due date and grace period
The due date is on your pawn ticket. After the due date there is a 90-day grace period to redeem or renew, with interest still counted each month.
