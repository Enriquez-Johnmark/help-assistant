# How pawning works

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## What pawning is
You leave an item of value, such as gold jewellery or a watch, at a branch and borrow money against it. You get the item back when you repay the loan and its interest.

## What you need
Bring the item and one valid ID. An appraiser checks the item and tells you how much you can borrow. You get the cash and a pawn ticket.

## Keep your pawn ticket
The pawn ticket shows the loan amount, the interest, the due date and the item. You need it to renew the loan or to get your item back.
