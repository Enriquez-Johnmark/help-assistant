# Paying bills

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How to pay a bill in the app
Tap Bills, choose the biller, enter your account number with that biller and the amount, and confirm with your PIN. Keep the reference number on the last screen.

## How to pay a bill at a branch
Bring your bill or your account number with the biller. Pay the amount plus a ₱10 service fee at the counter and keep the receipt.

## Fees
Paying a bill in the app is free for most billers. Some billers add their own fee, shown on the screen before you confirm.
