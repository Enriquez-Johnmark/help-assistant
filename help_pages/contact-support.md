# Contacting support

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How to reach us
Chat with us in the app under Help, or visit any branch. A person answers chats every day from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

## What to have ready
The reference number of the transaction, the date and time, and the amount. Never include your PIN, OTP or password in a message.

## What support can do
Support can check a transaction, look into a transfer that didn't arrive, help with a locked wallet, and take a complaint.
