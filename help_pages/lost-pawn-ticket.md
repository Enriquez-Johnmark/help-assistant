# If you lose your pawn ticket

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## What to do
Go to the branch where you pawned the item with one valid ID and report the lost ticket. Fill in an affidavit of loss form; the branch has a copy.

## Getting a new ticket
A replacement ticket is issued after 3 banking days, for a ₱50 fee. Until then, nobody can redeem the item with the old ticket.
