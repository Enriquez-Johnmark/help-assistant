# Fees for sending money

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Fees for branch pickup
| Amount sent | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₱1,000 | ₱15 |
| ₱1,001 to ₱5,000 | ₱35 |
| ₱5,001 to ₱20,000 | ₱80 |
| ₱20,001 to ₱50,000 | ₱150 |

## Fees for sending to a wallet
Sending from one Halimbawa Money wallet to another is free up to ₱10,000 a day. Above that, the fee is ₱10 per transfer.

## The most you can send
You can send up to ₱50,000 in one branch transfer. To send more, make another transfer on a later day or ask at a branch about the large-amount process.
