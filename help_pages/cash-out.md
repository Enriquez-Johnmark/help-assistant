# Taking money out of your wallet

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Ways to cash out
At any Halimbawa Money branch, by showing the cash-out code from the app and your ID, or by sending to a bank account.

## Fees
Cashing out at a branch costs ₱20 per cash-out. Sending to a bank account costs ₱15 per transfer.

## Cash-out codes
A cash-out code works for 1 hour. If it expires, make a new one in the app; the money stays in your wallet.
