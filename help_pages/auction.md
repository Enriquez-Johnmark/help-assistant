# Items that are not redeemed

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## When an item goes to auction
If a loan is not redeemed or renewed by the end of the 90-day grace period, the item may be sold at a public auction.

## Before the auction
Halimbawa Money sends a text message to the mobile number on your pawn ticket before the auction date. You can still redeem the item until the day before the auction.

## After the auction
Once an item is sold, it can't be returned.
