# Sending money to someone in the Philippines

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How to send money at a branch
Bring a valid ID and fill in the send form with the receiver's full name and mobile number. Pay the amount plus the fee at the counter. You get a receipt with a 10-character reference number. Send the reference number to the receiver; they need it to claim the money.

## How to send money in the app
Open the Halimbawa Money app, tap Send, choose Branch pickup or Wallet, enter the receiver's details and the amount, and confirm with your 6-digit PIN. The reference number appears on the last screen and in your history.

## Who can receive
Anyone with a valid ID can claim at any Halimbawa Money branch. A wallet user receives in their wallet straight away.
