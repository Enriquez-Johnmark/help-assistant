# Claiming money sent to you

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## What to bring
Bring the 10-character reference number and one valid ID with your photo. The name on your ID must match the receiver's name on the transfer.

## How long you have
Money waiting at a branch can be claimed for 30 days. After 30 days it is returned to the sender, who can claim it back with their receipt.

## If your name doesn't match
If the sender misspelled your name, the sender must ask to change the receiver's name at a branch before you can claim. See "Changing or cancelling a transfer".
