# Renewing a pawn loan

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## What renewing means
Renewing gives you more time to repay. You pay the interest due and the service charge, and the loan gets a new due date one month later.

## How to renew
Bring your pawn ticket to any branch, or renew in the app under Pawn, using your ticket number. You can renew a loan up to 4 times.

## Paying part of the loan
When you renew, you can also pay part of the loan. The interest for the next month is then counted on the smaller amount.
