# Keeping your account safe

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Your PIN and OTP are yours alone
Never share your PIN, your OTP or your password with anyone, including people who say they are from Halimbawa Money. We will never ask for them.

## If you think someone has your PIN
Change your PIN in the app at once, under Settings, then contact support. If your wallet was used without your permission, a person in support will look into it.

## Scams to watch for
Be careful with messages saying you won a prize, that your account will be closed, or asking you to click a link and log in. Halimbawa Money sends account messages only inside the app.
