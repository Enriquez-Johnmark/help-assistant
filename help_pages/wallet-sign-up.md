# Signing up for the wallet

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## What you need
A mobile number that can receive text messages, an email address, and one valid ID with your photo. You must be at least 18 years old.

## How to sign up
Download the Halimbawa Money app, tap Sign up, enter your mobile number and the OTP sent to it, and set a 6-digit PIN. Then verify your ID to use every feature.

## If the sign-up doesn't finish
Check that your app is up to date and that your phone has a steady internet connection. One mobile number can have only one wallet; if your number already has a wallet, log in instead.
