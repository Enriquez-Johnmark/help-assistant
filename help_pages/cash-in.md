# Adding money to your wallet

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Ways to cash in
At any Halimbawa Money branch, from a bank account linked in the app, or by receiving a transfer.

## Fees
Cashing in at a branch is free up to ₱8,000 a month; after that, the fee is ₱10 per cash-in. Cashing in from a linked bank account is free.

## When it arrives
Branch cash-ins arrive at once. Bank cash-ins arrive within 30 minutes on banking days.
