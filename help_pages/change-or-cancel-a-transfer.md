# Changing or cancelling a transfer

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Changing the receiver's name
The sender can change the receiver's name once, for free, at any branch, before the money is claimed. Bring the receipt and your ID.

## Cancelling a transfer
The sender can cancel a transfer that hasn't been claimed. The amount is returned in full; the fee is returned only if you cancel on the same day you sent it.

## Transfers sent to the wrong wallet
A wallet transfer can't be cancelled in the app once it is sent. Contact support with the reference number; a person will look into it. Never send your PIN or OTP to anyone who says they can reverse a transfer.
