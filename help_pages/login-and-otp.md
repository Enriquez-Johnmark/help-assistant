# Logging in and OTPs

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How logging in works
You log in with your mobile number and your 6-digit PIN. On a new phone, you also enter a one-time PIN (OTP) sent to your mobile number by text message.

## If the OTP doesn't arrive
Wait 2 minutes, then tap Resend. Check that your phone has signal and that your SIM is the one registered to your wallet. An OTP expires after 5 minutes.

## Never share your OTP
Halimbawa Money will never ask for your OTP or PIN, by text, call, chat or email. Anyone who asks for it is trying to take your money.
