# When a transfer says Pending

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## What Pending means
A transfer shows Pending while it is being checked. Most transfers finish within 15 minutes.

## If it stays Pending
If a transfer is still Pending after 2 hours, contact support with the reference number. Don't send it again: a second transfer could charge you twice.

## Money taken but not received
If your balance went down but the receiver hasn't got the money, a person in support needs to check your transaction. Contact support with the reference number and the time you sent it.
