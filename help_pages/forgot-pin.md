# If you forget your PIN

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## Resetting your PIN
On the login screen, tap Forgot PIN, enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number, then take a selfie to confirm it's you. You can then set a new PIN.

## If your wallet is locked
Entering the wrong PIN 5 times locks your wallet for 24 hours. After that you can try again or reset your PIN.

## A new mobile number
If you no longer have your registered mobile number, a person in support needs to help you. Contact support; never give your PIN to anyone.
