# How much you can borrow, and the interest

> Made up for a demo. Halimbawa Money is not a real company, and every fee, limit and time on this page is invented.

## How the amount is set
The appraiser weighs and checks the item. For gold jewellery you can usually borrow up to 70% of the appraised value.

## Interest
Interest is 3% of the loan for each month, counted from the day of the loan. A part of a month counts as a full month.

## Service charge
There is a ₱5 service charge for each new loan, renewal or redemption.
